Telemetry

With IMU enabled in the game, Splitter gets position, velocity and gyro rates at 60 Hz for your own drone. Per-gate speed and distance are always computed from the full stream; this controls what is stored per race for the flight-path and speed views.

samples per second (60 = everything, ~1 MB per race; 20 is plenty for a path)

Protocol log

Keeps the raw frames the game sends (IMU sampled to one every 5 s) so you can see exactly what arrives — e.g. what Nemesis mode emits. Browse it under Protocol.
